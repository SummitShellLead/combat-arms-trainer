## ✨ Features

### 🎮 Core Trainer
- ESP Outlines — see enemy players through walls
- No Recoil — remove weapon kick for steady aim
- No Spread — shots always land on the crosshair
- Rapid Fire — increase fire rate for all weapons
- Unlimited Stamina — sprint without tiring

### 🎨 Visuals & Overlay
- Player Names & Distance — readable tags above enemies
- Skeleton ESP — bone-based player outlines
- Color Picker — customize ESP and menu theme
- FPS Counter — lightweight performance overlay

### 🏋️ Practice & Training

- Damage Indicator — on-screen hit feedback
